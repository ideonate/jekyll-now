These days my time goes on two ventures of my own.

Stepthrough.ai

Help videos that never go stale.

Software changes every week, and the help videos that explain it go out of date the day they ship. Give Stepthrough a help article or a one-line brief, and an AI agent works through your product in a real browser to produce a finished narrated walkthrough with captions and chapters. When your UI changes, Stepthrough notices, takes the walkthrough again, and proposes an updated video for your team to approve.

For support, customer success and product teams at B2B SaaS companies. Live and invite-only.

Visit stepthrough.ai

Noodlet

A safe home for the lessons teachers make with AI.

A teacher drops in a quiz or simulation they made with Claude, ChatGPT or any other AI assistant, shares one link or QR code, and watches on a live board as each pupil works through it. Lessons run sealed off from the internet and stay private to the class; pupils join without accounts, and their names never reach Noodlet.

Free for teachers, with no advertising and no sale of pupil data.

Visit noodlet.com
