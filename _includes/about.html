About Dan

Dan Lester

Founder, Stepthrough.ai and Noodlet

I'm a software developer and entrepreneur in Cambridge, UK. I've co-founded three companies that were acquired.

I was the technical co-founder of Conversocial, a VC-backed enterprise SaaS company acquired by Verint (Nasdaq: VRNT) for $50m in 2021.

Earlier exits include a Facebook app agency (iPlatform) sold to larger competitor Betapond, and a bootstrapped WordPress plugin business (Google Apps Login) sold to a US-based portfolio company.

Since then I have worked on machine learning, open source data science tooling, and fractional CTO roles for startups. Now I'm building Stepthrough and Noodlet.

I am a pro bono mentor for the Founder Institute London accelerator programme and a member of the Computing School's Employer Advisory Group at Anglia Ruskin University.
